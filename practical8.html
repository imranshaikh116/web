About I2IT

I2IT is a premier institute for IT education located in Hinjawadi, Pune. Affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University, it offers UG and PG programs in technology and management.

  • Established: 2001
  • Location: Hinjawadi, Pune
  • Affiliation: SPPU

Courses Offered

BCA

3-year undergraduate program covering programming, databases, and web technology.

B.Tech (CS)

4-year engineering program in Computer Science and Engineering.

MCA

2-year postgraduate program in Computer Applications with advanced specialization.
